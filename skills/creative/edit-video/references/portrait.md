# Portrait delivery

Compose the full 9:16 frame from the brief and the project's visual language first. Establish the camera, graphic, and caption regions, then check them against the destination UI. Preserve the chosen composition while moving or reflowing the elements at risk. Footage and scene backgrounds fill their regions; protect the face, important actions, labels, and whole caption plates locally.

A project's graphics-above/speaker-below layout stays a stacked portrait composition. Crop the camera to fill its panel without stretching; keep the speaker prominent while the graphics teach above. A safe-zone guide is an overlay on that composition, not a smaller canvas to fit the entire video inside.

Select the platform, organic or paid placement, locale, and extra interface elements. Record a project-owned profile with canvas, evidence scope, UI reservations, caption lane, and app-verification status. Treat cover/profile-grid crops separately from playback overlays.

The bundled `safe-zone-profiles.json` preserves official **ad-template** measurements dated September 18, 2026. Use them for matching ad placements. For organic Shorts/Reels/TikTok, they are diagnostic references, not mandatory exclusions or a default shared canvas. Their intersection does not establish an organic safe area. When organic app evidence is unavailable, use modest project-owned text/control reservations, label them provisional, and retain the intended full-frame composition.

- YouTube Shorts has organic editor visual guides for overlap with buttons, descriptions, and device edges. Prefer those guides for its delivery check.
- Meta's 9:16 ad diagram includes top, bottom, side, and lower-right exclusions. Its large bottom reservation belongs to that ad context.
- TikTok's ad templates vary by anchors, caption length, and LTR/RTL region. Use the corresponding template for that placement.

For a shared organic export, accommodate the actual destinations' controls around the key content. Adjust a label, caption lane, or camera crop before reducing the entire composition. If verified UI differences require variants, keep the same editorial timeline and visual hierarchy. Do not combine unrelated ad exclusions into a blanket organic layout constraint.

Review the composition at phone size with guides hidden first: the speaker and explanation should occupy the intended panels and remain prominent. Then show the relevant guides and inspect faces, complete caption plates, labels, transitions, and moving objects. Passing a mask check does not compensate for an unreadable or empty composition.

Finally check the destination's draft/editor preview with actual post copy, native captions, and extra overlays. Record device, date, and result; mark app verification pending when access is unavailable. Keep guides out of the delivered file. Previewing does not require publishing.
