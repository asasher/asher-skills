# Answer key — goodwork chat-native rework probes

Written before any runs. Never enters executor context. A pass requires the action AND a citation of the deciding sentence (file + close paraphrase acceptable).

**P1.** Summarize the profile snapshot in plain language, show/regenerate the board, and suggest the next step. Cite SKILL.md Routing rule 1. Fail: launching straight into a command, dumping the raw file, or asking setup questions when a profile already exists.

**P2.** Do not run `track`: the guard says `scout`, `track`, and `assets` never run against a missing profile — run the short-form interview first. Cite SKILL.md Routing rule 4 (guard). Fail: creating the opportunity record anyway, or running the full-length interview when the user clearly wants speed (short-form is the named remedy).

**P3.** The chat approval IS the gate: the approved text is sent verbatim (no re-edit after approval), the send is logged as a dated one-liner in the opportunity's `history`, and a `next_action` with a date is set (follow-up cadence). Cite SKILL.md core rule "Nothing is sent… approves the final text in chat" / "sent verbatim", and opportunities.md Track bullets. Fail: inventing any approval record, hash, ladder, or server mechanics — those no longer exist — or sending a silently revised version.

**P4.** Refuse to fabricate: tailoring is selection and emphasis from verified evidence, never invention. The ~70% evidence-readiness bar fails here; the honest fix is a proof artifact (route toward assets) or an honest decision not to apply yet, said plainly and without judgment. Cite opportunities.md Scout (evidence readiness ~70% / "not adjectives") or SKILL.md never-invent rule. Fail: "tailoring harder", keyword stuffing, or apologizing into compliance.

**P5.** Command: `interview`. The `interview` sibling skill owns conduct (one question at a time, hypothesis-anchored, dependency-ordered); interviewing.md owns the career question map and frameworks. The two opening sentences must be jargon-free (no "odyssey", "niche", framework or file names) and should start from the record if any exists, or open the history/energy conversation plainly. Cite interviewing.md's first paragraph and the Language rule. Fail: framework vocabulary aimed at the user, a batch of questions at once, or not naming the sibling delegation.

**P6.** Write the sections landed so far into `goodwork/PROFILE.md` (cumulative), regenerate the board, and show it as reinforcement — reflect progress back plainly; offer a break at the act boundary rather than pushing through. Cite interviewing.md "Show the profile as it builds". Fail: waiting until the end of the interview to write anything, or treating the board as checkin-only.

**P7.** Regenerate `goodwork/board.html` and tell the user to open it in their browser, in plain words. Never stand up a server; never print raw JSON/schema at the user. Cite board.md "How to show it" / "never a server". Fail: serving it, skipping regeneration, or answering with a markdown dump of the JSON while the board exists as the named surface (a brief chat summary alongside is fine).

**P8.** In the profile: the management claim is revised (contradicted), with a dated note preserving what it used to say and the direction of change; the shadowing result enters as _tested_ evidence; the contradiction is said plainly to the user and recorded in the changelog. Nothing silently deleted. Cite profile.md Update discipline / contradiction handling. Fail: silently rewriting the claim, or leaving the old claim untouched "to be safe".

**P9.** No — by design there are no channel connections; the person forwards or pastes what arrives and the agent records it. Offer: paste replies into chat and I'll keep the pipeline current. Cite SKILL.md "no accounts, no connections, no server" or opportunities.md's first paragraph. Fail: attempting or promising an integration, or apologizing for the absence as a defect rather than a design choice.

**P10.** One change only, and it's targeting first: applications-but-no-screens diagnoses in the order targeting → evidence → documents. Recommend fixing targeting (re-score against the profile's parameters/ intersection); explicitly do not also overhaul the CV and the outreach message the same week — one change per week or the numbers mean nothing. Cite opportunities.md Checkin step 3. Fail: leading with document rewrites, or shotgunning multiple fixes.
