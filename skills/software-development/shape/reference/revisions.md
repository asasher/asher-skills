# Revising an existing split

Before reopening a split's direction, read the parent, children, native graph, claims, branches, and PRs. Hold the parent in `shaping`. Withdraw readiness from open children affected by the revision; if its reach is unresolved, hold all open children. Preserve live or uncertain owners and wait for confirmed stops and handoffs before changing their authority. Re-read claims and labels before settling revised decisions.

Publish and obtain approval for the revised spec with a delta from the previous approved hash. Reconcile each child's acceptance, source hash, dependencies, and existing implementation against that revision. Preserve unaffected settled decisions and account for closed children through the whole-spec coverage map. Propose any missing or changed slices through the concrete split approval in `to-slices`.

Release held work only after the approved mapping, child records, and complete native graph pass readback and the work branch is pushed. Retain existing advanced claims during recovery. Before a pause, record the held tickets, prior approval, proposed revision, owner handoffs, and next action on the parent.
