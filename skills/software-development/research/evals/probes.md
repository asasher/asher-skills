# Research probes

Pre-deployment probes with `SKILL.md` and `reference/research-contract.md` in context. Require an exact citation for each answer.

## Scenario

You are researching one question: "What delivery guarantees does the vendor's webhook system make?"

## Probes

**P1.** A well-known blog summarizes the guarantees. Is that enough?

**P2.** What must the dossier contain?

**P3.** One conclusion cannot be traced to a source. Can cautious wording keep it?

**P4.** The findings favor the integration. Do you decide to proceed?

**P5.** What do you return?

## Answer key

- **P1:** No. "Work from primary sources" and treat aggregators as discovery aids.
- **P2:** One self-contained HTML dossier with "the question, concise answer, findings, unknowns, contradictions, and as-of boundary." Every claim is cited.
- **P3:** No. The audit says, "Never soften an unsupported assertion into prose that merely sounds cautious." Repair it or name the exact gap.
- **P4:** No. "Leave the downstream decision open."
- **P5:** "The dossier path, concise answer, material unknowns and contradictions, as-of boundary, and audit result."

Pass bar: five of five on both executors.
