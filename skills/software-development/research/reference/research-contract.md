# The claim audit

Run over every sentence of the dossier that states a fact, an inference, or an unknown:

- Its class is visible: fact, inference, or unknown.
- A fact cites the primary source that owns it, or plainly labels the downgrade to a secondary source.
- The citation resolves to the cited source and identifies the relevant version, section, or line.
- An inference names the claims it rests on and stays within their scope.
- Time-sensitive material carries its as-of or accessed date.
- Conflicting sources are shown side by side. An unsuccessful search states its scope and leaves the claim unknown.

The audit passes only when every such sentence is traceable or explicitly unknown. Support each assertion or name the exact gap and its consequence. Judge quality by traceability.
