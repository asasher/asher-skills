# The claim audit

Run over every material sentence of the dossier before returning it:

- Its class is visible: fact/observation, inference, or unknown.
- A fact cites the primary source that owns it, or plainly labels the downgrade to a secondary source.
- The citation resolves to the cited source and identifies the relevant version, section, or line.
- An inference names the claims it rests on and does not exceed their scope.
- Time-sensitive material carries its as-of or accessed date; the dossier states its as-of boundary up front.
- Conflicting sources are shown side by side, never averaged away; failure to find a source is reported as a bounded search, never as proof of absence.

The audit passes only when every material statement is traceable or explicitly unknown. Never downgrade an unsupported assertion into prose that merely sounds cautious — repair it or name the exact gap and its consequence. Source count alone is not a quality signal.
