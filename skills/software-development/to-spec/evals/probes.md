# To-spec probes

Pre-deployment probes with `SKILL.md` in context. Require an exact citation for each answer.

## Scenario

A design conversation settled a driver-payout direction. Retry policy was discussed but never decided. Research and prototype projections are present in the shaping record.

## Probes

**P1.** Do you ask about retry policy?

**P2.** How must every Notes line be classified, and what does a blocking Note mean?

**P3.** A concrete source path is the only reliable pointer to an established pattern. May it appear in the spec?

**P4.** Where does the completed spec go, and does `to-spec` publish it or change the ticket?

**P5.** Must every spec contain a diagram?

**P6.** The record discusses three builds but never settles a split. Do you recommend one?

**P7.** What does the spec carry for the research dossier and prototype?

**P8.** What must the fidelity audit prove?

## Answer key

- **P1:** No. Undecided points "become Notes lines, never questions."
- **P2:** Blocking, delegated, or deferred. "A blocking Note means the direction is not ready to publish or build."
- **P3:** Yes. A concrete path is allowed when it is "the reliable pointer to an established pattern."
- **P4:** Write "an untracked scratch file" and return its path. The description says "no interview or publication"; no tracker mutation belongs to this skill.
- **P5:** No. Add one only when it "materially clarifies the direction," and verify that it renders.
- **P6:** No. Include a Recommended split only "when shaping settled one."
- **P7:** One Supporting artifacts entry per decision-informing artifact with its kind, question, one-line takeaway, and durable pointer.
- **P8:** Every current material decision, requirement, exclusion, assumption, and unresolved point is represented, and every spec statement traces to the settled record.

Pass bar: eight of eight on both executors.
