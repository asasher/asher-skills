# Spec and milestone waves

Resolve one approved spec ticket or one repository milestone. Read its tickets, approved split graph, claims, PRs, and blockers. A spec scope includes its descendants and the parent's coverage and promotion work. A milestone scope includes its assigned tickets and approved split descendants; milestone grouping preserves each ticket's branch target.

After a wave, re-read the scope and outcomes. Admit the next ready, unblocked frontier under the build capacity and ownership rules. Continue while selected workers can make progress; a completed launch alone is not a completed wave. Keep external prerequisites as reported blockers. Surface membership changes, replacements, and approval changes; expand beyond the selected scope only when the user selects that work.

A review-ready PR waits for the human's merge selection. Use `merge review` to present the PRs needed for the next wave; pass an explicit selection through `merge`. After confirmed merges and tracker readback, recompute blockers and branch bases. Children integrate into the spec branch before the parent's whole-spec verification and promotion. Existing workers retain their deadlines and consumed review budgets.

Continue independent eligible work when another ticket is blocked. When progress requires a merge selection, shaping, external work, an exhausted worker's next decision, or unavailable capacity/liveness information, report the wait and pause affected work. Observe each wave only through its recorded worker deadlines, bounded by any earlier user deadline. At expiry, checkpoint the wait and retain live or uncertain reservations. Honor stop requests. Before pausing, record the scope, completed work, live claims, pending PRs, blockers, and next action in this thread; worker checkpoints remain on their tickets/PRs. Resume by reconstructing actual tracker and worker state.

A spec is complete after its approved coverage is satisfied, promotion is confirmed merged, and its ticket is closed. An unsplit spec uses its ordinary delivery PR. A milestone is complete when every required ticket, approved descendant, and replacement is delivered and closed, or explicitly excluded by the user. Build delivery requires confirmed PR merges; split delivery includes promotion. Apply [milestone grouping](milestones.md) to verify this against both open and closed records. Distinguish delivery completion from the milestone's administrative closure, which follows the user's selection. An empty frontier, closed duplicate, or collection of open PRs alone establishes neither completion.
