The workflow

  1. 01

    Choose & inspect

    Pick the format and takes. Find the clean screen, microphone track, and camera sync.

  2. 02

    Edit the story

    Transcribe, choose the best takes, and remove repetition. Keep natural breaths and complete words.

  3. 03

    Clean the voice

    Reduce noise, balance the level, and compare against the original at equal speech loudness.

  4. 04

    Build the visuals

    Compose camera and screen. Add diagrams, timed motion, and captions for the chosen format.

  5. 05

    Render & review

    Check the export. You watch and listen, then accept it or mark changes.

The visual language

Refine the style from the existing Unroll video: dark panels, warm white type, and mint for the active idea.

  • One type family. Start with Arial; keep headings, labels, and captions consistent.
  • A small diagram vocabulary. Objects, groups, connections, selection, and a closer view.
  • Motion follows the explanation. Reveal, connect, highlight, or zoom when the spoken idea arrives.
  • Layout follows the format. Reflow diagrams for portrait and give each beat time to read.

These tools build on the previous edits. The reusable skill and comparison runner still need to be built. If FFmpeg cleanup fails the voice comparison, test a neural denoiser before adding it to the stack.

How we know it improved

Keep the known raw footage and old exports. After each skill change, rerender the same footage. Compare voice at equal speech loudness and graphics on the same cut. You choose A, B, tie, or neither. Then review the whole edit.

Start with a Short

Use Recap for voice cleanup and Unroll for motion. Keep the SDLC landscape video as a regression case. Accept the first treatments before making them defaults.
