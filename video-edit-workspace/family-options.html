What each skill owns

SkillResponsibilityResult
watch-videoUnderstand supplied video. Combine scene inspection with speech and timing when relevant. Preserve uncertainty.Timestamped scene map, speech evidence, selected frames. Understanding, not cut decisions.

    └── diagram-design             for applicable diagram structure and static assets

DESIGN.md                          shared visual decisions, read by the relevant skills

In this repo's vocabulary, watch-video and clean-voice are primitives. motion-graphics is a composite if it calls diagram-design. edit-video is a composite that delivers one video. An orchestrator only earns its place for a queue of videos, persistent scheduling, or a broader production loop.

The 3Blue1Brown part

The useful similarity is how the explanation unfolds: give the viewer an intuition, transform a persistent object, reveal the consequence, then hold the result. That needs more than animated diagram labels.

Use Manim for geometry, equations, algorithms, and continuous transformations. Use HTML/SVG + GSAP through Hyperframes for software diagrams, UI, text, and screen-oriented explanations. These are renderer branches inside motion-graphics, both governed by the same monochrome DESIGN.md. diagram-design keeps its static-first contract.

What to take from video-use

I reviewed browser-use/video-use at 9575612, including its editing instructions, timeline/render helpers, and bundled Manim instructions. This was a source review, not a runtime benchmark.

  • Useful: compact word-timed transcripts, a frame/waveform/word timeline view, edit decision lists, and rendered-cut inspection.
  • Adapt: its ElevenLabs transcription path to our local MLX Whisper path unless diarization or another requirement justifies a different provider.
  • Do not inherit as defaults: fixed fades, two-word uppercase subtitles, compulsory parallel agents, or a changing dominant colour in every scene.
  • Keep human review: sampled frames and waveforms do not prove that speech sounds natural or an explanation is understandable.
