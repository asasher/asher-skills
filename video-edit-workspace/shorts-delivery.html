Caption treatment

  • Sentence case, short phrases, normally one or two lines. Start at 48 px on a 1080-wide portrait canvas.
  • Use final edited speech for timing. Check technical terms, negations, reading time, and cut boundaries.
  • Keep the entire caption plate clear of UI, faces, and the diagram. Hold its position within a shot.
  • Keep a clean master and timed sidecar. Make the burned-in Short version from that same timeline.
  • Check native or automatic subtitles so two copies do not unintentionally overlap. Subtitle upload support differs by platform.

Landscape is a separate treatment

Keep the normal 64 px edge padding at 1920 × 1080. Prefer a timed sidecar unless burned-in captions are wanted.

Landscape players can show temporary playback controls and native subtitles. Allow for those, plus any requested end-screen elements, without applying the large Short-feed exclusions to the whole video.

Cover crops are another check

Check the profile grid, cover, and feed preview separately. A title that clears playback controls can still disappear in a cropped thumbnail.

The workflow inside edit-video

  1. Choose format and destination. Record placement, locale, caption strategy, and any extra interface overlays.
  2. Compose with a profile. Pass the usable region and caption lane to motion-graphics before scenes are built.
  3. Render with review guides. Check the longest captions and moving graphics at phone size. Guide layers stay out of the export.
  4. Check the destination preview. Use actual controls, post copy, and caption settings. Record device and date; mark untested platforms as pending.
  5. Run human review. Score caption accuracy, overlap, legibility, voice, and whether the scene teaches the idea. Pin the profile revision alongside the footage and skill revision.

Voice stays on the stronger local DeepFilterNet treatment you preferred, with a bypass comparison when reviewing new footage. Relighting remains deferred.
