03

Groom the whole backlog. Shape one ticket.

Consolidate tickets when their decisions belong together. Transfer approved native prerequisites and dependents to the survivor and read them back before closing sources. Preserve live ownership, advanced capture state, and approved decisions.

Unique ticket after consolidationRouteWhy it is there
Clear outcome, observable acceptance, delegated implementation choicesready-for-agentCan be implemented without shaping
Product, scope, or design decisions remainneeds-shapingOffer a separate shaping thread
Reporter facts are missingneeds-infoWait for the missing information
Requires a human actionready-for-humanAgent cannot complete the remaining action

01 / Ticket

Establish the record

Capture a ticket if needed. Establish shaping ownership and withdraw readiness before reopening decisions; hold affected split children until their owners stop and revised authority is settled.

02 / Decisions

Users → behavior → design

Ask material unresolved questions; carry settled answers and delegated choices forward. Research facts and publish inspectable prototype comparisons when judgment is needed.

03 / Approval

Pin the spec revision

Publish HTML and record the user's approval against the exact spec revision.

04 / Release

One ticket or a split

Release the ticket, or let to-slices approve, wire, and read back the child graph from its approved spec. Prior approval carries when it covers the exact revision and plan.

Present the delta when a spec changes; approval stays bound to the published revision. Reconcile held children and the whole coverage map before release. An unrelated research commit leaves unchanged spec approval intact.

Testing batches use milestones

A milestone such as “User testing — September 10” groups distinct findings and holds shared session notes. Each issue moves through groom, shape, and build on its own.

Capture proposes or reuses the milestone with your issue list. Groom preserves membership when merging reports; slices inherit it. Milestone membership groups progress; approved spec splits determine integration branches.

Groom and status surface completed batches. Closure follows your selection after checking remaining items and linked replacement work. An issue has one milestone, so conflicting assignments need a decision.

05

One owner builds, fixes, and brings the proof

The owner adopts a new building reservation assigned to its launch and uses implement, adversarial-review, and prove-your-work inline. Independent review checks the work; verification independence scales with risk.

Choose the build scope
CommandWhat it buildsWhen it ends
backlog buildThe ready, unblocked frontier at invocation. Seven tickets with capacity three are drained as slots free.Initial tickets reach review-ready PRs or recorded stops. Newly unblocked work waits for another invocation.
backlog build spec 100The approved spec, its child waves, integration, and promotion. A bare approved spec ticket selects the same scope.Approved coverage is satisfied and promotion has merged, or progress needs outside action.
backlog build milestone "User testing"Milestone tickets and their approved split descendants, in successive waves.Required delivery is merged and closed across the group, including replacements and split promotions, with explicit exclusions; otherwise report the wait.
Between waves. Observe results → present needed PRs → human selects merges → confirm tracker state → build the next frontier.

The coordinator continues independent eligible work while other tickets wait. Capacity waits keep tickets queued; expired worker or user deadlines leave a resumable checkpoint. Human merge selection, shaping approval, branch targets, worker budgets, and ownership remain in force. A review-ready PR is still awaiting merge; an empty frontier alone does not mean the spec or milestone is complete. Milestone closure follows your selection.

Successive waves and recovery ↗
Browser checks stay isolated from the user's desktop

Use headless Playwright by default. An alternative must demonstrate app access, control, and capture on the execution host. Give each concurrent run separate browser sessions, auth state, fixtures, and capture paths.

A headed fallback needs an isolated display or explicit approval to use the user's session. Unavailable capabilities leave claims unverified. Reuse tests and helpers; direct tool checks record actions and observations. Temporary scripts belong to run evidence, while durable regression tests stay in the suite.

Verification means trying the behavior

Drive the app through the changed journey. Test the pinned head with its intended base, recording the tested commit and integration tree. Reuse normal-risk check results only when revisions, tree, commands, environment, and fixtures match; independent verification runs its own checks. Preserve exact commands, output, script content, and fixtures. Look at the screenshots. Use recordings or GIFs when interaction proves the claim.

A check can pass, fail, be pre-existing, or remain not verified. Missing access never becomes a passing verdict.

A resume keeps the bound

Default review bounds are three checking passes and one hour, within the ticket's outer deadline. Record consumed passes and the next actor. A fix, new machine, or late defect cannot silently restart the loop.

After writers stop, product questions return to shaping; environment, verification, or budget stops needing a human return to ready-for-human. Unknown liveness retains the building reservation. A split parent accounts for every approved criterion and routes missing slice-sized work through capture before promotion.

06

Suggest broadly. Merge the human’s selections.

Merge review reads all open PRs and groups ready, blocked, stale, and dependent work. A shortlist does not authorize a merge.

01 / Selection

Name the PRs

The human chooses. Order prerequisites before dependents.

02 / Freshness

Check head and base

Require risk-appropriate independent reports, proof, required CI, and the tested integration tree. Head-specific waivers require explicit human authorization.

03 / Merge

Confirm MERGED

Require current proof and green checks; re-read head/base just before merging against the verified head. Honor configured branch protection, while allowing unprotected repositories. Compare the merged tree with the tested tree and verify unexpected results before dependent work continues. A queued merge is still pending. Confirm ticket closure too.

04 / Cleanup

Remove finished work

Stop its stack; remove a safe secondary worktree and finished work/artifact branches.

Keep the published record

One published evidence report owns per-claim detail. The PR keeps its summary, checkpoint, and current report link; the ticket keeps the pointer and outcome. Reports retain reproducible commands and temporary scripts after branch cleanup.

Preserve unfinished work

Retain branches with dependents, active claims, or unique unmerged work. Keep a spec branch until promotion. Dirty and unknown files require inspection; cleanup never switches or removes the primary checkout.

07

The fifth verb closes the learning loop

A note is cheap enough to record during any run. A retro pass uses the evidence across runs to decide what needs fixing.

1

Review recent sessions

Start with the latest three completed builds or shaping sessions. Persist the selected versions before reading or discussion; a resume finishes that pending batch before selecting newer sessions.

Find concrete friction

Read the selected transcripts for corrections, tooling failures, verification gaps, and recovery trouble.

2

Check existing issues

Compare findings with open and closed project issues, including their comments.

You select the action

Review proposed issues or comments. Accept, revise, or dismiss each finding before any tracker write.

3

Keep learnings in the backlog

Accepted findings become project issues or evidence on existing issues. The resolver decides whether upstream escalation is useful.

Checkpoint the reviewed batch

After discussion and selected writes finish, advance contiguous completed coverage and retain unfinished pending versions in .retro/checkpoint.json in the primary checkout. It stays outside Git; worktrees share it and machines keep separate copies. Offer another batch when sessions remain.
