05

One owner builds, fixes, and brings the proof

The owner uses implement, adversarial-review, and prove-your-work inline. Independent review checks the work; independence for behavioral verification scales with risk.

RiskBehavioral verificationCode reviewCompletion evidence
Light
No executable or operational effect		Relevant artifact checks; explain why behavior is inapplicableIndependent reviewerCurrent-head checks and evidence
Normal
Routine behavioral change		Owner drives the changed surfaceIndependent reviewerPer-claim proof and inspected visuals
High
Auth, money, destructive data, cross-surface interactions		Separate verifier; isolated runtime fixturesSeparate reviewerFailure-path and data-safety proof where applicable

Verification means trying the behavior

Drive the app through the changed journey. Preserve exact commands, output, script content, and fixtures. Look at the screenshots. Use recordings or GIFs when interaction proves the claim.

A check can pass, fail, be pre-existing, or remain not verified. Missing access never becomes a passing verdict.

A resume keeps the bound

Default review bounds are three checking passes and one hour, within the ticket's outer deadline. Record consumed passes and the next actor. A fix, new machine, or late defect cannot silently restart the loop.

Product questions, incomplete verification, and exhausted bounds remain explicit stops. An open PR is not yet review-ready.

06

Suggest broadly. Merge the human’s selections.

Merge review reads all open PRs and groups ready, blocked, stale, and dependent work. A shortlist does not authorize a merge.

01 / Selection

Name the PRs

The human chooses. Order prerequisites before dependents.

02 / Freshness

Check head and base

Require independent review, proof, applicable verification, and required CI.

03 / Merge

Confirm MERGED

Pin the selected head. A queued merge is still pending. Confirm ticket closure too.

04 / Cleanup

Remove finished work

Stop its stack; remove a safe secondary worktree and finished work/artifact branches.

Keep the published record

Published HTML and evidence URLs remain. Temporary branches are not an archive. Evidence reports retain the commands and temporary script contents needed to reproduce their claims.

Preserve unfinished work

Retain branches with dependents, active claims, or unique unmerged work. Keep a spec branch until promotion. Dirty and unknown files require inspection; cleanup never switches or removes the primary checkout.

07

The fifth verb closes the learning loop

A note is cheap enough to record during any run. A retro pass uses the evidence across runs to decide what needs fixing.

1

Review recent sessions

Start with the latest three completed builds or shaping sessions. Later sweeps take the next three new or updated sessions.

Find concrete friction

Read the selected transcripts for corrections, tooling failures, verification gaps, and recovery trouble.

2

Check existing issues

Compare findings with open and closed project issues, including their comments.

You select the action

Review proposed issues or comments. Accept, revise, or dismiss each finding before any tracker write.

3

Keep learnings in the backlog

Accepted findings become project issues or evidence on existing issues. The resolver decides whether upstream escalation is useful.

Checkpoint the reviewed batch

After the discussion, record coverage in .retro/checkpoint.json in the primary checkout. It stays outside Git; worktrees share it and machines keep separate copies. Offer another batch when sessions remain.
