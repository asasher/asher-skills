One trip through the lifecycle

How you interact with a repo that has the asher-skills SDLC set installed — from a raw idea to merged code. Columns show who is holding the work at each step.

The shape of the experience: you are hands-on while shaping (a conversation), absent while building (autonomous pipelines), and personally hold exactly four decisions: confirm the batches, bless the spec, approve the split, authorize the merge. Everything else runs without you.

You ⇄ shape · conversation

The interview — shape asks, you decide

interview settles the strategic decisions one frontier at a time; domain-modeling pins terms and records ADRs as they land. This is the part of the lifecycle that genuinely needs you present.

hands-on
to-subagent · dispatch

research & prototype

Questions that need sources get a cited dossier; questions paper can't settle get a throwaway artifact. Both run without you and report back to the thread.

AFK-ok
to-spec · automatic

Settled subject → spec on its ticket

Crystallises without being asked: the spec lands on the subject's ticket, diagram first. The thread then watches the spec'd tickets for your comments — even if you've walked away.

You · decision

Bless readiness

Comment on the ticket or in the thread. Nothing proceeds to splitting until you say the spec is right.

gate 2 of 4
to-tickets · on your approval

Split into tracer-bullet tickets

Quizzes you on granularity and blocking edges, then publishes born-shaped children in dependency order. The spec'd parent is superseded.
